What's in here. 155 living species from the Plants of the World Online list at Kew. Named hybrids (such as Freeman maple, Acer × freemanii) and about 70 fossil species known only from rock are left out.
How the leaves are drawn. Each drawing is generated from the species' traits, so it shows the leaf's character rather than a traced photograph. For 89 species the leaf traits are well documented. For 55 lesser-known Chinese and Southeast Asian species, the shape follows the group (series) the species belongs to, and the sheet says so. For 11 species only the native range could be confirmed.
Sources. Plants of the World Online, Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew; Maple Society classification (Davis, 2021); Flora of China.
