## 2026-11-14 — The owner's changes after using the build (F1-32 to F1-36)
The owner tried the Focus tab in the simulator and asked for five changes, recorded as F1-32 to F1-36 in the F1 design's §2.2.
- **F1-32, End early asks twice:** the sheet's way out (Gentle's "End session", Steady's 3-second hold) no longer ends the session. It leads to a breath in the session's place (`EndBreathView`, `EndBreathModel`): "Breathe in" for 4 seconds and "and out" for 4, once for Gentle and twice for Steady, with a disc that fills and empties the dial. Then "Still want to end?", with "Keep going" and "End session"; the session ends only there. Gentle's 5-second ring and Steady's 15-second "Ending in 15s" countdown are gone. Why: the ring was too slight to notice, and the owner wanted the choice put again after the pause.
- **F1-33, turning the dial:** the marks follow the finger between lengths (`DialSpread`, `MinuteDial.spread(_:follows:)`), and a step is taken halfway through its 30° (`DialTurnTracker`), so the dial settles on the nearer length. The length still moves in 5-minute steps. Why: the ring jumped from one length's marks to the next, and nothing moved with the finger.
- **F1-34, the Focus tab's backdrop:** still stars and a low glow in the season's tint behind every Focus screen (`FocusSky`). One still frame, so a long session never redraws it. Why: the running screen was a dial on an empty page.
- **F1-35, session buttons:** "End early" is a glass capsule beside "Take a break" (`SessionActionRow`), and End early's sheet is only as tall as its content, over the session screen's own dark instead of the navy sheet surface. Why: "End early" was small text with no button, and the sheet was half empty and off-theme.
- **F1-36, strictness in a free build:** the strictness chooser stays in the preset editor in a build without Family Controls. Nothing changes in the code.

## 2026-10-29 — The owner's five answers to the F1b final review (F1-27 to F1-31)
The owner decided five behaviours the final review raised, recorded as F1-27 to F1-31 in the F1 design's §2.2.
- **F1-27, the Live Activity's break line:** "Study · break until 14:28" on the Lock Screen and in the expanded view, read aloud as "Study, break until 14:28", in the app's clock format. Why: with Maple suspended nothing updates the activity when a break ends, so "Break · back at 14:28" went false and "break until" stays true. The app's own break screen and the Herbarium's mini bar, which Maple updates itself, are unchanged.
- **F1-28, an intention swiped away:** closing intention entry without Return saves the field as Return would (trimmed, at most 60, none when blank); a cleared field still removes it at once (`IntentionDraft.savedOnDismiss`). Why: typed words shouldn't vanish with a swipe.
- **F1-29, the free build's plain timer:** in a build that can never block, the first "Use a plain timer · it won't count" is remembered (`AppSettings.plainTimerChosen`, decoded tolerantly), and the Focus tab then goes straight to the plain timer's start screen, whose "Plain timer · apps aren't locked" keeps it honest. Builds that can block still reset the choice at each start, since Screen Time may be turned on. Why: the "can't lock apps" card before every session said nothing new. DEBUG: `-MapleDemoPlainTimerChosen YES`.
- **F1-30, a plain timer's strictness:** Gentle, whatever its preset. One rule, `Strictness.applying(to:)` (and `FocusPlan.appliedStrictness`), decides it for the start screen, Home's Start, the receipt's Again and every way out (`SessionEndPath(_:)` on the plan, so Release all apps too). A plain timer starts with one tap, shows no Locked line, and ends through Gentle's End early; "Hold to start as a plain timer" is gone. Why: a plain timer locks nothing, so Locked's and Steady's exit costs mean nothing for it. The ledger still records the preset's own strictness. A session that lost its block mid-session locks nothing any more; it only keeps its plan's way out.
- **F1-31, a receipt shown the next day:** "saved · 2h 10m that day", with that day's total, only when the session's Maple day is earlier than the shown day and the shown day's window has opened (wake − 60, later after a short night, `CollectionSnapshot.livingDay(at:context:)`). Between bedtime and that moment it still says "today", so a finish past bedtime or a receipt opened in the small hours never says "that day". Its spoken form and large-text line match, and the breakdown's cap follows ("Over that day's 6h · this didn't add"). Today's receipts keep "today". Either way the numeral opens the sheet on the day of the total it showed (`ReceiptModel.savedDay`): as "Saved today" from a receipt saying "today", and as "Saved that day" from one saying "that day", where the cap's reason reads "Over that day's 6h" and an empty day reads "Nothing saved that day." Why: "today" was false the next morning, and "that day" was false the same night; and a receipt about last evening's 40m opened a sheet about this morning's nothing. (The window's opening was added on 2026-10-30: the first version moved at bedtime. "Saved that day" was added on 2026-11-12: until then a receipt saying "that day" opened the sheet on today.)

Affects: `FocusActivityState`, `FocusActivityAttributes`, the widget; `IntentionEntry`; `AppSettings`, `FocusView`, `FocusStartView`, `NeedsScreenTimeView`; `StartScreenModel`, `FocusStartLines`, `RunningSessionModel`, `RunningSessionView`; `ReceiptModel`, `ReceiptView`; the F1 design's §1.1, §5.2, §5.3, §5.6, §5.7, §5.8, §5.11, §5.12, §5.13 and §5.16.

## 2026-10-24 — Reduce Motion keeps the gold finish, as a cross-fade
Under Reduce Motion a session's finish keeps its tone and loses only its motion. Every counted mark's gold rises together over 0.6 s, holds to 1.2 s and is gone by 1.8 s, with no stagger and no travel; the finish is white when nothing counted (or the session ended early). There is no sweep, ripple or pulse, the opened leaf is at its final size at once, and the words cross-fade in on their usual timings. `FinishTimeline.settledTime(markCount:reduceMotion: true)` is 1.8 s. Why: §4.6 removes motion, not the tone, and gold over counted time is what the finish says (F1-18); this changes the earlier plan text, which left the gold out under Reduce Motion. Affects: `FinishTimeline`, `ReceiptView`, `-MapleDemoReduceMotion` screenshots, and spec Overrides item 7.

## 2026-10-24 — Home's numeral cross-fades between a session's time and the saved numeral
Home's numeral still rolls between two saved values, as in M1, but never while a session runs: a running session's time changes crisply each second, and a switch between a session's time and the saved numeral, either way, is a cross-fade (`StatNumber.Change`: `.roll`, `.crossFade`, `.none`). The Focus streak in Home's header changes by opacity, not a numeric roll. Why: a numeric roll animates a blur, and nothing on an F1 surface animates a filter (§4.6); a session's end must never roll "0:01" into "2h 10m". This changes the earlier plan text, which had the numeral roll. Affects: `StatNumber`, `HomeHeader`, Home's day and session states.

## 2026-10-24 — The Live Activity: a sketch, words from MapleCore, and upkeep by a pure plan
The running session's Live Activity (F1 design §5.11) is display only: no buttons, no push and no App Group. The `MapleWidgets` extension links only `MapleActivity`, so everything it draws arrives in the activity: the next hours leaf as a `LeafSketch` (byte pairs in a 0…255 square, at most 240 outline points, under 2 KB; thinning keeps each outline's extreme points, so every species stays within 1 unit of its box, which `LiveActivitySketchTests.keepsShape` checks over all 155), the season tint as a plain colour (never gold), and every line already worded by `FocusActivityState.lines(locale:calendar:)` in MapleCore ("until 14:52 · Study", "Study · Chemistry ch. 4", "Break · back at 14:28", "Session complete"). The Lock Screen shows the preset name only; the intention, capped at 40 characters, appears only in the expanded view, and reaches the activity before `start` returns (`FocusSessionStore.resendLiveActivity()`). `LiveActivityPlan` (MapleCore, tested) decides the upkeep: end every activity that isn't the running session's, update the running one when its content changed, and ask for one only when `LiveActivityAsks` (MapleCore, tested) allows: once per session per launch. A dismissal blocks it for the rest of the launch; a refusal is asked again once, after Maple next comes to the front (`mapleBecameActive()`). `ActivityKitController` applies it after every store change, on launch, every foreground and every 15-second refresh. Why: the widget can't link MapleCore or MapleDesign, and ActivityKit's `Activity` isn't `Sendable`, so only values cross between them. Affects: `MapleKit/Sources/MapleActivity/`, `Extensions/MapleWidgets/`, `App/Features/LiveActivity/`; the version numbers live in `Base.xcconfig` so the extension's match the app's; `check-release.sh` scans both binaries; the layouts are checked by simulator screenshots (`Scripts/shoot-activity.sh`), and the minimal view only in review, because it shows only when two apps' activities compete.

## 2026-10-24 — Focus copy lives in MapleCore (P3)
Every Focus string and decision is a MapleCore value (`FocusCopy`, `FocusSheetCopy`, `FocusSettingsCopy`, `SelectionText`, and the screen models `StartScreenModel`, `FocusStartLines`, `RunningSessionModel`, `ReceiptModel`, `SavedTodayModel`, `StreakWeek`, `FocusActivityState.lines`), tested with Swift Testing; views only lay them out. ## 2026-10-24 — The first session is remembered (P4)
`AppSettings.hasStartedFocus`, saved, turns true when the first session starts. Until then the start screen preselects Quick, shows the counts line's definition and "You can lock your phone. Maple keeps time.", and the first start asks once for notification permission if iOS hasn't asked yet (F1-23). Why: the hints are for a first session only, and a settings value survives relaunches without reading the ledger. Affects: the Focus start and running screens. A member who reinstalls sees the hints again.

## 2026-10-24 — A receipt is shown once (P5)
`AppSettings.lastReceiptSeen` holds the last receipt seen. `ReceiptPicker.pending` returns the newest session that ended within the last 24 hours and isn't that one, and the receipt marks itself seen as it appears. "Ended at" is `min(end.placed, plannedEnd)`, because an end event is stamped when the app noticed it. The finish plays only within 10 minutes of the end. Why: a session that ended while Maple was closed still gets its receipt, once, and an old one never resurfaces. Affects: the Focus tab's routing and a session-end notification's tap. An ended-away receipt older than a day is never shown.

## 2026-10-24 — The session store refreshes every 15 seconds while a session runs (P6)
`FocusSessionStore.refresh()` runs on launch, every foreground, when protected data becomes available, every 15 seconds while a session runs, and at the planned end and the break end (`FocusRefresh`). The dial ticks every second from `FocusSessionStore.now` (trusted time) without a refresh. Why: each refresh reads the ledger and the block back, and doing that every second would cost battery for nothing. Affects: a lost block is noticed up to 15 seconds late. A Live Activity's intention doesn't wait for a refresh: the start sends it again (`resendLiveActivity()`).

## 2026-10-24 — Every Focus demo argument is a typed Core argument (P7)
Every Focus argument in F1 design §8.2, the Focus `-MapleDemoPresent` values included, is a typed `DemoLaunchOptions` field, so any of them keeps the launch in demo stores and seeds its scenario. `-MapleDemoTab focus` stays app-level, so with nothing else it shows the live Focus tab. Why: one place parses and tests the arguments, and a Focus screenshot never touches the member's files. Affects: `docs/debug-launch-arguments.md`. `-MapleDemoShip f1` still seeds the Focus demo after F1 shipped.

## 2026-10-24 — The streak week starts on the locale's first weekday (P8)

## 2026-10-10 — Sleep leaves come only from Apple Health (D9)
The M1 sleep categories (Nights tracked, On-time bedtimes, Bedtime runs, Steady weeks, Seasons in rhythm, Steady mornings, Full nights) are earned only from Apple Health's nights, as rewards.json's data sources say and spec D9 settles: there is no manual self-report fallback. Why: a tap is self-report, so a leaf earned by tapping would mean nothing, and the leaves are worth having only because they reflect real sleep. Affects: the Goodnight tap in wind-down mode only logs the phone-down time (wind-down's own state and Home's "Morning, no Apple Health" numeral) and feeds no counter; `NightLedger` judges `SleepNightObservation`s from M1's importer (`CollectionStore.recordSleep(_:at:)`), each in the time zone the night was spent in; without Apple Health these leaves show "Needs Apple Health" and are left out of the next leaf, buds, Almost there, pacing and Profile totals, and Home's next-leaf row offers "Turn on Apple Health" (spec Overrides #4). Full nights also need asleep time (a Watch, a tracker or another app). HealthKit never reveals read access, so finishing Apple's prompt counts as connected.

## 2026-10-10 — One count rule: "N of 155" counts opened leaves
Every surface that shows how many leaves the member has (Home's caption, Tree view, the Herbarium's count, owned bar, "Earned" chip and section counts, Profile, onboarding, VoiceOver) counts opened (placed) leaves only. Waiting leaves are buds and are mentioned apart ("1 waiting"), never added to N. Why: the count and the tree must agree; a leaf counts once it is on the maple. Affects: `LeafCount` (MapleCore) is the single rule, read through `CollectionStore.leafCount` / `openedCount`; the tree's stage name follows the same N (spec §5.2).

## 2026-10-10 — A morning with no sleep data shows tonight's wind-down start
When there is neither last night's sleep from Apple Health nor a Goodnight time, Home's morning numeral is tonight's wind-down start as a locale-aware clock time ("22:15" / "10:15 PM") with "wind-down tonight · bed 23:00", never a countdown. Why: a 14-hour countdown in the morning reads as a chore, and "0" or "—" would break spec §1.12 (every no-data case has a designed state). Affects: `NowState` (`Content.windDownTonight`) and spec §3.4's "Morning, no data for last night" row; the day still counts down to wind-down until focus ships.

## 2026-10-10 — "% of members who have this leaf" uses CloudKit
Each leaf shows the share of Maple members who own it (like Opal gems). Counts live in Maple's CloudKit public database: only anonymous per-leaf unlock counts leave the device, never health data. No third-party processor. Needs the paid Developer Program, so until then the app shows "Not enough members yet" (never a made-up number). The stats sit behind a `LeafOwnershipStats` protocol so the backend can change.

## 2026-10-10 — Leaves are milestone rewards; catalogue is the supplied 155 species
Every species in `MapleCore/Resources/species.json` (supplied catalogue, 155 living *Acer* species) is a one-time reward unlocked by a specific milestone, like Opal's gems. Leaves are drawn natively by a Swift port of the supplied `leaf-generator.js`, so they can appear in any colour and size. Reference material lives in `Catalog/`.

## 2026-10-10 — Build output lives outside the repo
The repo sits in `~/Documents`, which iCloud Drive syncs. iCloud adds file-provider attributes that make `codesign` reject build products ("resource fork, Finder information, or similar detritus not allowed"). Scripts build into `~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedData/Maple`, which is also Xcode's default.

## 2026-10-10 — Hand-written Xcode project
No project generator is installed. `Maple.xcodeproj` is kept minimal: synchronized folders (new files under `App/` are picked up automatically), build settings in `Config/*.xcconfig`, code in the `MapleKit` package. Validate edits with `xcodebuild -list` and a build.

## 2026-10-10 — iPhone only, iOS 27 minimum
Scope is iPhone only (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`), minimum iOS 27. Gives access to `PrivateCloudComputeLanguageModel` and avoids availability branches.
