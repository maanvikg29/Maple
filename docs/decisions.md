## 2026-11-26 — The syrup van: the economy's rules (logic only, no screens yet)
The owner approved the syrup van design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-syrup-van-design.md`) with "yes to all, drop the tab bar". This entry covers its step 2, the economy; nothing is on screen yet.
- **Leaves.** 0.9 leaves for every minute focused (54 an hour), rounded up when the session ends: the owner's numbers, given the same day and replacing first one leaf per 5 minutes and then 0.8 a minute. The pace is kept as whole leaves an hour (`WorkshopTuning.leavesPerHour`), so 0.9 a minute is exact.
- **Only a session that reaches the end of its timer earns.** A session ended before then (End early, Release all apps, and the emergency release while strictness is still in the code) loses every leaf it would have earned, however long it ran; it stays in History as ended early, with its time focused. The owner's rule, 2026-11-26: Pip falls out of the tree and the session's progress is lost. Time saved toward the special leaves is untouched: an early end still keeps its counted minutes there (F1-1). Every session earns the same way, from its time focused (its length without breaks), whether it blocks apps or is a plain timer; so a free-tier build without Apple's Family Controls permission plays the whole game. Time saved, with its daily limit, still decides the special leaves. A session removed from History keeps what it earned. The running session's leaves arrive when it reaches its end.
- **No strictness levels in the van's timer.** The owner, 2026-11-26: "no strictness feature". Gentle, Steady and Locked go when the timer screen is rebuilt (step 3), with the chooser, the hold to start and the emergency release; every session then starts with one tap and ends one way. This replaces F1-36 (the chooser staying in a free-tier build). The code on `main` still has the levels until then.
- **No balance is stored.** `workshop.json` holds what the member did (cooked, spent, each machine's level), the leaves earned so far, and the ledger's total when the van last looked. Each look adds only what the ledger's total has grown by, so a leaf can't be counted twice, and a short read of the ledger can't take leaves away.
- **A retune never touches leaves already earned.** The ledger's total depends on the pace and the earning rules it is counted with, so the van saves both beside the total. When either changes, it starts a new count from the ledger as it now reads: earned leaves stay, the same sessions are never paid twice, and the next session earns as usual. (Found by the final review: comparing against one saved number would have paid out, or held back, hours of leaves after a change of pace.)
- **Syrup is counted in tenths of a bottle**, so an upgrade can add a fraction without rounding. One leaf is one bottle before any upgrade.
- **Upgrades.** Two machines, the evaporator and the filter press. Each level costs leaves and adds syrup per leaf; the machines' bonuses add up. Cooking uses the yield at the moment of cooking; syrup already made never changes.
- **The numbers are in one place,** `WorkshopTuning.starting`. The ladders' prices and bonuses are starting values: the design leaves the pace for its own pass (§14).
- **What the screens must not do** (the owner, same day): the timer never shows a leaf count or a rate; the leaves falling from the tree while a session runs are an animation only, random, and don't match the count; there are no presets (no Study, Quick, Deep, Read), only the clock and one block mode. These belong to steps 3 and 4.
- Spending syrup, decorations, special syrups and every screen come in later steps.

## 2026-10-24 — Reduce Motion keeps the gold finish, as a cross-fade
Under Reduce Motion a session's finish keeps its tone and loses only its motion. Every counted mark's gold rises together over 0.6 s, holds to 1.2 s and is gone by 1.8 s, with no stagger and no travel; the finish is white when nothing counted (or the session ended early). There is no sweep, ripple or pulse, the opened leaf is at its final size at once, and the words cross-fade in on their usual timings. `FinishTimeline.settledTime(markCount:reduceMotion: true)` is 1.8 s. Why: §4.6 removes motion, not the tone, and gold over counted time is what the finish says (F1-18); this changes the earlier plan text, which left the gold out under Reduce Motion. Affects: `FinishTimeline`, `ReceiptView`, `-MapleDemoReduceMotion` screenshots, and spec Overrides item 7.

## 2026-10-24 — The Live Activity: a sketch, words from MapleCore, and upkeep by a pure plan
The running session's Live Activity (F1 design §5.11) is display only: no buttons, no push and no App Group. The `MapleWidgets` extension links only `MapleActivity`, so everything it draws arrives in the activity: the next hours leaf as a `LeafSketch` (byte pairs in a 0…255 square, at most 240 outline points, under 2 KB; thinning keeps each outline's extreme points, so every species stays within 1 unit of its box, which `LiveActivitySketchTests.keepsShape` checks over all 155), the season tint as a plain colour (never gold), and every line already worded by `FocusActivityState.lines(locale:calendar:)` in MapleCore ("until 14:52 · Study", "Study · Chemistry ch. 4", "Break · back at 14:28", "Session complete"). The Lock Screen shows the preset name only; the intention, capped at 40 characters, appears only in the expanded view, and reaches the activity before `start` returns (`FocusSessionStore.resendLiveActivity()`). `LiveActivityPlan` (MapleCore, tested) decides the upkeep: end every activity that isn't the running session's, update the running one when its content changed, and ask for one only when `LiveActivityAsks` (MapleCore, tested) allows: once per session per launch. A dismissal blocks it for the rest of the launch; a refusal is asked again once, after Maple next comes to the front (`mapleBecameActive()`). `ActivityKitController` applies it after every store change, on launch, every foreground and every 15-second refresh. Why: the widget can't link MapleCore or MapleDesign, and ActivityKit's `Activity` isn't `Sendable`, so only values cross between them. Affects: `MapleKit/Sources/MapleActivity/`, `Extensions/MapleWidgets/`, `App/Features/LiveActivity/`; the version numbers live in `Base.xcconfig` so the extension's match the app's; `check-release.sh` scans both binaries; the layouts are checked by simulator screenshots (`Scripts/shoot-activity.sh`), and the minimal view only in review, because it shows only when two apps' activities compete.
