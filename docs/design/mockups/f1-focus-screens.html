Section 3 · The Focus screens

Every screen is built around the minute-marks dial you picked. The same dial sets the length, runs the session, counts down a break, fills while you hold and becomes the receipt, so the whole tab feels like one object. Things to try: turn the dial on the first phone (or click it and use the arrow keys), tap the chips, and press and hold Hold to start, Hold to end and Hold to release.
