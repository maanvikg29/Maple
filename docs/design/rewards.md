# Leaf rewards: design notes

Generated by the rewards architect, stress-tested by a skeptic, finalised 2026-10-10. Machine-readable table: `MapleKit/Sources/MapleCore/Resources/rewards.json`.

## Model
Every one of the 155 species is a permanent reward, like an Opal gem. Each is pinned to exactly one (category, threshold) rung, and no two leaves share a rung. There are 19 categories, each counting one healthy behaviour. All counters are cumulative high-water marks that never go down, except the live bedtime run, which is short (top 30), forgiving and never the home screen's auto-picked target. A leaf unlocks once and is never lost. Availability: 73 leaves at M1 (sleep only), 104 after M2, 133 after M3, 144 after M4, 151 after M6 and 155 at M9.

WHAT CHANGED FROM THE DRAFT (every high- and medium-severity skeptic finding applied):
- Sleep without a Watch. A source ladder: (A) Watch or hardware asleep stages, then (B) other apps' asleep samples, then (C) iPhone 'In Bed', labelled 'estimated from iPhone'. iPhone-only members can earn every sleep leaf except the 10 full-night leaves, which show 'Needs Apple Watch or a sleep tracker'. Onboarding detects which sources exist and walks the user through Apple's Sleep schedule and 'Track Time in Bed with iPhone'.
- Focus can't be idled or clock-hacked:
  - monotonic timing
  - planned length of 90 min or less
  - a confirm tap, or a 25-min credit cap
  - 3 h/day and 10 h/week caps
  - the wind-down window is excluded
  - from M3, unblocked sessions count half toward hours
  The top focus leaf moved to a new focus-weeks ladder (A. pentaphyllum at 68 weeks), so even a 4 h/day focuser needs about 16 months.
- Screen Time selections must 'count for leaves': a category, or 3+ apps with real daytime use. Changes apply after 7 days, nights with a same-day shield edit are excluded, and missing callbacks are neutral.
- Bedtime runs are capped at 30 and redefined as 'at least 5 of every 7 nights on time'. Untracked nights count as slips; early nights are neutral. In simulation the 30-night run has a median of day 65, p90 day 161 and 0% never. The draft's 60-night run had p90 day 775 and 31% never; even a 1-slip rule left 12% never reaching 30. The three run evergreens moved to steady weeks.
- Phone-free mornings count from actual wake (neutral outside wake −30/+15 min). The reveal opens 30 min after waking, and no morning notification is sent.
- Sound nights became 'sound wind-downs': the night must also be on time (or scroll-free), and the sound must start no later than bedtime + 30 min.
- Mood check-ins became 'reflection weeks' (3 check-ins in a week), so there is no daily tap habit.
- M1 ladders were densified and re-timed by simulation. Evening installs count the same night, and late Watch data is re-evaluated for 72 h (upgrade only).
- All 18 ladder tops are documented (k) species. The 11 placeholders (u) sit on mid-to-late rungs of 11 different ladders, and the five near-identical Monspessulana placeholders are on five different ladders.
- Rarity is now opt-in and aggregate-only, using DAP/Prio with Privacy Pass rate limiting (see percent_owned).
- Health- and Screen Time-derived unlocks stay on device.

PLACEMENT:
- Famous species sit on first rungs: sugar, red, silver, black, Japanese, Fullmoon, Nikko, field, sycamore, Norway, trident, paperbark, Montpellier.
- Distinctive forms mark round milestones: box elder (the only pinnate leaf) at 30 nights, Bigleaf at 25 full nights, Shirasawa's 11 lobes at 160 on-time nights, and A. pentaphyllum (the only palmately compound leaf) at the top of focus weeks.
- Each ladder is a family:
  - North American maples for nights tracked
  - Japanese/Palmata maples for bedtimes
  - trifoliates for runs
  - evergreens for steady weeks and seasons
  - northern and mountain maples for mornings
  - broad Acer-series leaves for full nights
  - Norway-maple relatives for focus
  - snakebarks plus the paperbark ('scroll' bark) for screen habits
  - Mediterranean maples for phone-free mornings
  - Himalayan maples for rituals
  - multi-region maples for community
- All 24 evergreens are on long-run consistency ladders (13 steady weeks, 6 seasons, 3 steady mornings, 2 full nights), so 'stays green' means 'kept it up'.

TIERS are journey labels, not rarity. The schema keys first-steps / common / uncommon / rare / legendary appear in the UI as First steps / Early / Seasoned / Devoted / Lifetime. They are derived automatically from the simulated median unlock day for a committed member:
- First steps: up to 8 days
- Early: up to 45 days
- Seasoned: up to 180 days
- Devoted: over 180 days
- Lifetime: the top rung of a ladder whose median is over a year
That gives 12 / 30 / 47 / 49 / 17. Henry's maple (top of the 30-night run) and the one-rung Sugar maple are exempt from Lifetime. The measured '% of members' is the only rarity shown, so the UI never says 'Rare · 41%'.

SEASON COLOUR is presentation, not a separate collectible. The tree shows each species in the real season's colours, and the herbarium card keeps the season the leaf was earned in, so a winter unlock stays frosted.

NEXT LEAF is a sticky target. It changes only when that leaf unlocks or the user pins another (Opal-style pin). It is chosen among locked leaves measurable for this member, excluding run leaves and unshipped categories, by fewest estimated days. Estimators are per category:
- daily counts: remaining ÷ 14-day rate
- weeks: remaining ÷ 8-week rate
- seasons: steady weeks this season against the 8 needed and the weeks left, otherwise the next season's end
- focus hours: remaining ÷ capped weekly rate
Before 14 days of data, rates are blended with the committed-profile prior. Ties go to the higher % complete. The herbarium adds an 'Almost there' row (top 3), and the live bedtime run shows as its own line on home.

UNLOCK DELIVERY: unlocks from the evening and overnight (sleep, wind-down, shield, sound, rituals, caffeine) are held for the morning reveal, which opens no earlier than 30 min after waking. Focus unlocks appear when the session ends, unless that is inside the wind-down window. At most 3 reveals per session; the rest wait in a 'New leaves' tray.

ENGINE: MapleCore ships a static, versioned rewards table (speciesId → category, threshold) next to species.json. LeafEngine replays deterministic counters from events and awards Unlock records keyed by species id, with date and season, so re-imports and merges never duplicate.
- Unlocks from sleep, sound, reflection, caffeine and Screen Time categories stay in the device-only localHealth store (encrypted device backup; re-derivable from HealthKit).
- Only first-steps, focus, ritual and community unlocks sync via CloudKit. Record this in docs/decisions.md.
- CI enforces that after shipping, thresholds only go down, a species moves only within an unshipped category, and new species become new top rungs.
- The pacing simulation below ships as a LeafEngine unit test, with M1-only, all-features, casual, heavy-focus and iPhone-only profiles that assert the targets.

M3 FALLBACK: if the Family Controls distribution entitlement is refused, or is still pending when M4 ships, the 29 Screen Time species are re-homed while no one can have progress toward them:
- Paperbark becomes the first wind-down ritual leaf.
- The Mediterranean maples move to a new M6 'gentle wake mornings' category: up within 10 min of Maple's alarm, at most one snooze.
- The snakebarks become new top rungs of rituals and sound wind-downs.

SIMULATION: 400 runs per profile from a 10 Oct 2026 start, with calendar weeks and northern seasons. Results are stable for Dec, Mar and Jun starts. The committed profile matches the draft's, with weekend clustering added:
- tracks 93% of nights
- on time on 89% of tracked weeknights and 78% of Fri/Sat nights
- steady wake after 80% of on-time nights, 50% otherwise
- full nights 75% / 40%
- focus on half of days, for 30–100 min (about 3.8 h/week)
- sounds on 35% of nights
- scroll-free 60%
- phone-free on 60% of mornings with a qualifying wake (about 42% overall)
- shield kept 50%
- mood check-ins on 45% of days, caffeine curfew 40%, rituals 35%
- two challenges a month, each completed 30% of the time

## Tree
The tree is the collection made visible, with daily feedback on top so it still feels alive between milestone unlocks.

1. STRUCTURE grows with species unlocked, measured against what this member can earn. It is a procedural Canvas maple, seeded per user so its silhouette is personal and stable. New stages add branches without moving existing ones.
- 'Earnable' (E) means species in shipped categories whose data source or permission this member has. For example, no Watch removes the 10 full-night leaves, and not opting into community removes 4.
- Stages: Seedling (0–3 leaves), Sapling (4+), Young maple (at least 20% of E), Spreading maple (45%), Old maple (75%), Grand maple (every earnable leaf).
- The stage is a high-water mark, so granting a permission later (which raises E) never shrinks the tree.
- Simulated committed member: Young about day 30, Spreading about day 115, Old about day 324, Grand at completion (about 21 months).
- M1 sleep-only member: Young about day 17, Spreading about day 89, Old about day 324.

2. SLOTS: every unlocked species gets a permanent slot at a branch tip, filled in unlock order from the crown outward. It shows as a small cluster of 3–5 copies of that species' own leaf, drawn by the native generator at 10–18 pt from pre-rendered sprites, so 155 species read as a varied canopy. Placeholder (u) species appear as a faint pressed-specimen outline, matching their herbarium card. Tapping a cluster opens that leaf in the herbarium.

3. BUDS: one soft amber bud sits at the slot where the home screen's next leaf will grow, and swells with progress (0–100%). The target is sticky and its counter never decreases, so the bud never shrinks. Two smaller, static 'resting' buds mark the next two candidates. If the user pins a run leaf, its bud uses the best run since pinning, while the live run is shown as a number.

4. TRUNK girth grows on a log scale with active weeks (weeks with at least one counted behaviour in any category), so every kind of member grows rings, with or without a Watch.

5. VITALITY comes from the last 7 nights: the share of on-time and full nights, plus scroll-free wind-downs from M3. Untracked nights are neutral (left out, not counted as bad). Members with no sleep source use focus and screen behaviours instead. Vitality changes only light and motion:
- warmer moonlight glow
- leaf scale between 94% and 100%
- a gentle sway
A rough week makes the tree dimmer and stiller. It never has fewer leaves, never wilts and never shows a warning.

6. SEASON follows the real date and the user's hemisphere:
- Spring: fresh light green, with tiny red maple flowers at the bud.
- Summer: each species' own summer green.
- Autumn: each species turns its own autumn colour (crimson, scarlet, orange or gold). Evergreens stay green.
- Winter: deciduous clusters take the frosted treatment (pale, rime-edged) but stay on the branch, never bare. Evergreens stay deep green with a little snow, so the 24 evergreen leaves visibly stand out.
The herbarium card permanently keeps the season the leaf was earned in.

7. UNLOCK MOMENT: during the morning reveal (from 30 min after waking) or after a daytime focus session, the bud opens over about 1.6 s. The leaf drifts down to its card, where the species is drawn large, the scientific and common names show, and the '% of members' line fades in. It then settles back into its slot. One soft haptic; no confetti and no sound by default. Under Reduce Motion there is no sway or drift, only crossfades.

8. CALM AND BATTERY:
- Each species × season is pre-rendered once, at 2 sizes, to a cached image. Canvas draws images rather than about 775 generated paths.
- Sway runs at 30 fps or less and pauses in Low Power Mode.
- From wind-down start until wake the tree is static (no animation), so Maple never invites late-night lingering.

9. ACCESSIBILITY: VoiceOver reads a summary, for example: 'Your maple: young maple, 34 species, 34 of the 145 you can earn. Next leaf: Acer glabrum, Rocky Mountain maple, 14 of 20 steady mornings. Bedtime run: 5 nights.' Dynamic Type scales the labels around the tree, not the drawing.

## Wellbeing and anti-gaming rules
- Leaves are permanent. Every counter is a cumulative high-water mark, so a recomputation (new iPhone, Health still syncing, revoked access) never shows less progress, and an earned unlock is never revoked. The one exception is the live bedtime run: it is capped at 30, forgiving, and shown without loss messaging ('New run: 2 · best 13').
- Bedtime runs are forgiving: a run continues while at least 5 of every 7 nights are on time. Untracked nights count as slips, so removing the Watch never helps. Early nights (asleep more than 2 h before bedtime, for example when ill) are neutral. Run leaves are never auto-chosen as the home target. After 3 weeks of mostly late Fri/Sat nights, Maple gently offers a separate weekend bedtime instead of nudging harder.
- Per-period caps everywhere: at most one count per night, day or week in every category. Focus sessions are planned at 10–90 min and timed with a monotonic clock. They need a confirm tap within 10 min of the planned end; otherwise at most 25 min is credited. Focus is capped at 3 h/day and 10 h/week, with no credit inside the wind-down or sleep window. From M3, unblocked sessions count at half rate toward focus hours. Focus days and focus weeks reward frequency and rhythm, not volume, and copy celebrates rest days.
- Sleep counts nights, not hours. A full night is a threshold (sleep need − 30 min, with need clamped to 7–9 h), measured on the main sleep episode only, so naps and extra hours earn nothing.
- Sleep sources are explicit. User-entered samples never count. Other apps' asleep samples count for leaves but are excluded from rarity reporting. iPhone In Bed data is labelled 'estimated from iPhone' and doesn't unlock full nights. A leaf that needs hardware the member lacks shows 'Needs Apple Watch or a sleep tracker' instead of 0% progress, and it is left out of that member's tree stage, next-leaf choice and rarity reporting.
- No leaf for time spent in Maple: nothing is earned for app opens, notification taps, sharing, browsing the herbarium or looking at the tree, and opening the morning reveal earns nothing. Mood counts as reflection weeks (3 check-ins in a week), not a daily tap streak. Sleep sounds count only on nights that were also on time (or scroll-free).
- Nothing is purchasable or premium-gated. All 155 species can be earned on the free tier, and blocking stays free under App Store guideline 4.10. Premium may only add cosmetics and never speeds up progress.
- No backfill: counting starts on the install night (if setup finishes before sleep), so years of HealthKit history can't unlock dozens of leaves at once. That history still appears in Insights.
- Schedule edits apply from the next night (cut-off 18:00 local), so a target can't be moved after the fact. Only the very first schedule applies immediately, and only if it is saved at least 30 min before its bedtime. Any schedule is allowed, including shift work and per-weekday schedules: Maple rewards your own rhythm, not a prescribed one.
- Late data is handled fairly: each night is evaluated provisionally in the morning and re-evaluated for 72 h as Watch data arrives, and results can only upgrade. Deterministic ids for nights and unlocks mean re-imports and merges never double-count.
- Screen Time leaves need a selection that counts: at least one category (Social and Entertainment are preselected), or 3+ apps with real daytime use. Selection changes apply after 7 days, and nights with a same-day shield edit are excluded. Windows must be monitored from their start; a missing start or end callback makes a night neutral, not failed. One rolling activity per window type stays under DeviceActivity's activity limit. Revoking Screen Time permission pauses these categories without penalty.
- Phone-free mornings are measured from your real wake time, and mornings outside wake − 30 to + 15 min are neutral. Sleeping in is never rewarded, and scrolling before your official wake time doesn't count as phone-free.
- Rewards never arrive at night. Evening and overnight unlocks are held until at least 30 min after waking. There is no morning push notification; a quiet lock-screen widget may say '1 leaf is waiting'. Wind-down notifications never mention leaves or progress, and the tree is static from wind-down until wake.
- Celebrations stay calm: no confetti, no sound by default, one soft haptic, and at most 3 reveals per session (the rest wait in a 'New leaves' tray). Reduce Motion is respected.
- Mood check-ins count the same whatever the mood, so a hard day earns as much as a great one. Caffeine curfew only evaluates journaled days and never penalises logging caffeine.
- Rarity is never framed as a competition. There are no individual leaderboards and no 'you're behind X% of members' messages; only leaf-level aggregates are shown. Tier names are journey labels (First steps, Early, Seasoned, Devoted, Lifetime), so 'Rare · 41%' never appears.
- Community challenges are opt-in, can be completed solo and use the same capped categories. Only 4 leaves depend on them. Members who don't opt in can still reach Grand maple, because tree stages count earnable leaves.
- Catalogue and table changes never take progress away. The rewards table is versioned, and CI enforces that after shipping, thresholds can only go down, a species can move only while its category is unshipped, and new species become new top rungs. LeafEngine keeps unlocks from any table version, and taxonomy changes (for example A. undulatum being merged into A. hyrcanum) keep earned leaves.
- Honest presentation: 'i' leaves carry an 'Approximate leaf' label with a one-line explanation. 'u' leaves get a distinct pressed-specimen treatment (sketched outline, herbarium tag), so look-alike placeholders read as intentional. Locked leaves show a silhouette with the exact rule and live progress. Percentages are never fabricated.
- The collection itself is private. Unlocks derived from health or Screen Time data stay in the device-only store, because a species id like amamiense implies '460 on-time nights'. Only first-steps, focus, ritual and community unlocks sync via iCloud.

## % of members statistic
MEANING: the share of Maple members who shared stats last month and have earned this leaf, as in Steam or Opal. It appears on locked and earned leaves in the herbarium and on the unlock card ('12% of members have this'). The detail view adds the base: 'of 4,300 members who shared stats in September'.

CONSENT: strictly opt-in. Onboarding asks once, in plain language ('Share which leaves you have, anonymously, so everyone can see how rare each leaf is'). It is off by default, with a toggle in Settings › Privacy. Opted-out members still see everyone's percentages; they just aren't counted. This replaces the logged 2026-10-10 'uses CloudKit' decision: CloudKit public-database records carry the creator's user record ID, so they cannot be anonymous. Update docs/decisions.md.

WHAT THE PHONE SENDS: once per calendar month, at a random time in the first 7 days after the month ends (via BGAppRefreshTask or the next foreground), through an Oblivious HTTP relay so no server sees the IP address. The report contains:
- the rewards-table version and the month
- one DAP report (IETF Distributed Aggregation Protocol, Prio3SumVec with 1-bit entries, length 310): an 'owned' bit and a 'counted' bit for each of the 155 leaves. 'Counted' is 0 when that leaf's category data came only from unverified sources (other apps' sleep samples), or when the leaf needs hardware the member lacks. Those members then neither inflate nor deflate that leaf.

The vector is split into two secret shares, each encrypted to a different aggregator: Maple's leader (a small Cloudflare Worker or Supabase function) and ISRG's Divvi Up as helper. Each share alone looks random, so no server, Maple included, ever sees one member's leaves. A zero-knowledge validity proof rejects malformed reports. Only per-leaf totals for a whole month, over at least 1,000 reports (DAP's min_batch_size), are ever revealed.

NEVER SENT: an account, Apple ID or device ID; the IP address; any time finer than the month; counts; health or Screen Time values; unlock dates.

ABUSE LIMITS WITHOUT LINKING: a token issuer, separate from both aggregators, checks an App Attest assertion once a month. It then issues exactly one blind-signed Privacy Pass token (RFC 9576/9578) per attested key per month. The report carries the token, which the leader checks and spends. Because the token is blind-signed, the issuer can't connect it to the report, and the aggregators never see App Attest keys. This replaces the draft's DeviceCheck parity bits, which couldn't enforce one report per week.

PUBLISHING: a monthly job collects the aggregate and applies isotonic regression down each ladder, so rung k is never shown as more common than rung k−1. It then writes a static rarity.json to a CDN containing:
- the rewards version and the month
- the member count, rounded down to the nearest 100
- per leaf: {state, pct, lo, hi}, where lo/hi is a Wilson 95% interval that treats sharers as a sample of all members

DISPLAY RULES:
1. Gate: no percentage anywhere until a month has at least 1,000 accepted reports. Until then every leaf shows 'Rarity appears once 1,000 members share stats', plus the count so far once it passes 100.
2. Structural zeros: if a leaf's fastest possible unlock (from its per-day and per-week caps) is longer than its category has been live, show 'No one yet' without computing anything. For example, no one can have 560 tracked nights within 18 months of M1.
3. A leaf with fewer than 10 owners is never shown as a number. It shows 'Under X%', where X is the smallest clean step (0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 3 or 5%) at or above the interval's upper bound.
4. Otherwise a number appears only if the interval's half-width is no more than 25% of the estimate: a whole percent at 2% or above, one decimal between 0.1% and 2%. If that fails, show the tightest bound the data supports ('Under 3%' at about 1,000 members).
5. Values are clamped to 'Under 0.1%' (only once a month has at least 10,000 reports) and 'Over 99%'. Never 0% or 100%.
6. A shown number changes only when the new month's estimate moves by more than its interval, so there is no 1–2 point jitter.
7. Category not shipped yet: 'Not earnable yet · arrives with Screen Time'.
8. Newly added species: 'New, rarity next month'.
9. Leaves that need hardware the viewer lacks still show the percentage, with 'Needs Apple Watch or a sleep tracker' beside it.

FALLBACK if no DAP partner is available: local differential privacy. Each month the phone reports one randomly chosen category's ladder level using k-ary randomized response, memoised per (category, level) as in RAPPOR's permanent randomized response, so repeated reports can't be averaged out. The gate rises to 5,000 and the same display rules apply.

CLIENT: fetches rarity.json at most once every 24 h on foreground (using ETag) and caches it on disk with its fetch time. UI states:
- Loading: a neutral placeholder line with no number, and no shimmer under Reduce Motion.
- Offline or stale: the last known value, with 'as of September' in the detail view.
- Never fetched: 'Rarity shows when you're online'.
- Not enough members: as in display rule 1.
- Opted out: everyone's percentages are still visible.

DISCLOSURE: the App Store privacy label says 'Health & Fitness: not linked to you, not used for tracking'. Add a privacy-policy section and a short DPIA. Copy never claims 'health data never leaves the device'. It says: 'Raw health and Screen Time data never leave your iPhone. If you opt in, Maple shares which leaves you have, split so no single server can read it, and only monthly totals across at least 1,000 members are published.'

## Open questions for the product owner
- Rarity backend: is it OK to run DAP (an open IETF standard) with ISRG's Divvi Up as the second aggregator? It also needs a small Maple leader (a Cloudflare Worker or Supabase function), a Privacy Pass token issuer and an OHTTP relay. This replaces your logged 'uses CloudKit' decision, because CloudKit public records carry the creator's user record ID and can't be anonymous. The cheaper fallback is local differential privacy, which needs about 5,000 sharing members before any number can appear.
- Rarity is now strictly opt-in, as the skeptic recommended for App Store guideline 5.1.3 and GDPR Art. 9. Are you OK that percentages may take months to appear (it needs 1,000 sharing members in one month)? Should the waiting state show the running count ('620 of 1,000 so far')?
- Rarity denominator: all members who shared that month (Steam-style; applied now)? Or only sharers who have that feature turned on ('12% of members using Screen Time')? With the first, Screen Time leaves look rarer, because many people never turn Screen Time on.
- Before M3, M4, M6 and M9 ship, should leaves from those categories appear in the herbarium as locked silhouettes labelled e.g. 'Arrives with Screen Time' (all 155 visible, which builds anticipation), or stay hidden until the feature exists?
- With every feature in use, month one gives about 32 leaves, above the 20–25 target, because each of the 11 non-sleep behaviours has one welcome leaf in its first two weeks. The M1 launch hits 20. Keep this, or push those welcome leaves later? Pushing them later would weaken the hook when M2 and M3 launch.
- Bedtime runs now use 'at least 5 of every 7 nights on time' (2 slips a week) rather than the brief's 'one missed night per week'. With a 1-slip rule, 12% of committed users never reached a 30-night run in two years. Is 2 slips right? Do you also want an 'Away' pause for trips or illness (set in advance, up to 7 nights, a few times a year)?
- From M3, unblocked focus sessions count at half rate toward focus hours (the skeptic's anti-idling fix). This slows members who never grant Screen Time. Keep it, or rely on the confirm-tap rule alone?
- iPhone-only members: on-time is estimated from the iPhone's In Bed start, which in practice means 'phone put down by bedtime + 30 min'. Is that acceptable, presented as 'estimated from iPhone'? Or should these members only get leaves that don't depend on sleep onset?
- Counting starts on the install night, with no HealthKit backfill. Do you want a small welcome backfill (e.g. the last 7 nights count toward Nights tracked), or keep it strictly from tonight?
- Thresholds: is 'asleep between bedtime − 2 h and bedtime + 30 min' the right on-time window, and is ±30 min right for steady mornings? Should sleep need stay clamped to 7–9 h? How should users under 18 be handled (an 8–10 h need, an age gate)?
- 77 species have no English name. Show only the italic scientific name plus range (recommended, since nicknames would be invented), or add gentle descriptive nicknames?
- Community challenges now hold 4 leaves (down from 5), so a member who never opts in can collect 151, and their Grand maple counts earnable leaves only. Is that acceptable, or should those 4 leaves also have a solo route?
- Referrals were 'legendary frosted' in the original leaf plan. Now that species come only from behaviour, should referrals give a cosmetic card treatment (not a species), or nothing?
- Two choices to confirm. Pinning a goal leaf on home (Opal-style) is included and makes the target sticky. The season a leaf was earned in stays as a permanent card treatment (winter unlocks stay frosted).
- Should mood check-ins and the caffeine journal stay device-only, as proposed? Mood is health-adjacent and guideline 5.1.3(ii) applies. The alternative is syncing them via iCloud like focus sessions.

## Categories
- **First steps** (`first_steps`, step, from M1): One-time: the first sleep schedule (target bedtime, wake time, wind-down length) saved in onboarding or Settings. Counts once, ever. Ladder: 1
- **Nights tracked** (`nights_tracked`, nights, from M1): Distinct nights after setup with a main sleep episode of at least 3 h from an accepted source. Source ladder, best available per night: (A) asleep stages from Apple Watch or an allow-listed hardware tracker (HKSource/HKDevice); (B) asleep samples from other apps (counted for leaves but excluded from rarity reporting); (C) iPhone 'In Bed' samples from Apple's Sleep schedule, labelled 'estimated from iPhone'. Samples marked HKMetadataKeyWasUserEntered never count. A night runs 18:00 to 18:00 local and belongs to the evening. The main episode is the longest cluster of samples, with gaps under 60 min merged; naps are ignored. The install night counts if setup finished before the episode began. Each night is evaluated provisionally the next morning and re-evaluated for 72 h as late Watch data syncs; results can only upgrade. The counter is a high-water mark. At most 1 per night. Ladder: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100, 160, 240, 330, 440, 560
- **On-time bedtimes** (`on_time_bedtimes`, nights, from M1): Tracked nights whose sleep onset falls between target bedtime − 2 h and target bedtime + 30 min. Onset is the start of the main episode's first asleep block of 20 min or more (sources A/B), or in-bed start for source C (estimated from iPhone, which in practice means the phone was put down by then). Onset more than 2 h early (for example when ill) is 'early': neutral, never counted against you. The target is the schedule in force at 18:00 that evening, with weekday and weekend schedules respected. The very first schedule applies immediately if saved at least 30 min before its bedtime. At most 1 per night; cumulative. Ladder: 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 45, 65, 90, 120, 160, 210, 270, 350, 460
- **Bedtime runs** (`bedtime_run`, nights in a run, from M1): On-time nights in your current run. The run continues while at least 5 of every 7 nights are on time: up to 2 slips (late or untracked) in any rolling 7 nights add 0 and are forgiven, and a 3rd slip within 7 nights ends the run. Untracked nights count as slips, so taking the Watch off never helps; early nights are neutral. A leaf unlocks when the live run reaches its threshold. The progress bar shows the live run, with your best run as a quiet 'best: 13' marker. Capped at 30. Run leaves are never auto-picked as the home 'next leaf' (the run appears as its own line); a user can pin one. Ladder: 3, 7, 14, 21, 30
- **Steady weeks** (`steady_weeks`, weeks, from M1): Calendar weeks (using the locale's first weekday) with at least 5 on-time bedtimes, counted when the week closes. Early and untracked nights are neutral. Cumulative. Ladder: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36, 44, 52, 60, 67, 74
- **Seasons in rhythm** (`seasons_in_rhythm`, seasons, from M1): Meteorological seasons for your hemisphere that close with at least 8 steady weeks. North: spring Mar–May, summer Jun–Aug, autumn Sep–Nov, winter Dec–Feb; flipped for the south. The hemisphere comes from region or time zone and is editable. A week belongs to the season that contains its Thursday. A season counts when its last week closes, and a season joined partway still counts if it reaches 8. Cumulative. Ladder: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
- **Steady mornings** (`steady_mornings`, mornings, from M1): Mornings whose final wake falls within ±30 min of that day's target wake time. Final wake is the end of the main episode's last asleep block (sources A/B), or in-bed end for source C. At most 1 per day; cumulative. Ladder: 3, 10, 20, 40, 60, 85, 115, 155, 220, 320, 420
- **Full nights** (`full_nights`, nights, from M1): Tracked nights whose main episode has total asleep time of at least your sleep need − 30 min. Sleep need is set in onboarding (default 7 h 30 min, adjustable 7–9 h). Naps and extra hours add nothing. Needs asleep-stage data (sources A/B). With iPhone in-bed data only, these 10 leaves show 'Needs Apple Watch or a sleep tracker' and are left out of that member's tree stage, next-leaf choice and rarity reporting. Ladder: 3, 12, 25, 40, 60, 90, 130, 190, 270, 370
- **Focus hours** (`focus_hours`, hours, from M2): Credited minutes from in-app focus sessions. Each session needs a planned length of 10–90 min and is timed with a monotonic clock (ContinuousClock), so changing the device clock does nothing. If ended by a tap (in the app, a Live Activity or a notification) before or within 10 min after the planned end, it is credited the time focused, up to the planned length. If it isn't confirmed within 10 min, credit is capped at 25 min. Sessions under 10 min credit nothing, and minutes inside your wind-down or sleep window never count. Caps: 3 h per day and 10 h per calendar week. From M3, sessions with Screen Time blocking count in full and unblocked sessions count at half rate here; focus days and focus weeks still count them fully. Stored as minutes, shown as whole hours. Ladder: 4, 18, 35, 55, 80, 105, 135, 170, 210
- **Focus days** (`focus_days`, days, from M2): Calendar days with at least one focus session that credits 25 min or more on its own (same session rules as focus hours). At most 1 per day; cumulative. Ladder: 1, 14, 28, 45, 65, 95, 140, 200
- **Focus weeks** (`focus_weeks`, weeks, from M2): Calendar weeks with at least 3 focus days, counted when the week closes. At most 1 per week, so even a full-time focuser can climb at most one rung step per week. Cumulative. Ladder: 4, 9, 15, 22, 32, 44, 56, 68
- **Sound wind-downs** (`sound_winddowns`, nights, from M2): Nights where a Maple sleep sound played for at least 15 min, starting between wind-down start and target bedtime + 30 min, and the same night was on time (or, from M3, the wind-down was scroll-free). Longer or louder playback adds nothing. Revealed the next morning. At most 1 per night. Ladder: 5, 15, 30, 50, 80, 130
- **Scroll-free wind-downs** (`scroll_free_winddowns`, nights, from M3): Wind-down windows (wind-down start to target bedtime) that were monitored from their start by Maple's rolling DeviceActivity schedule, ended, and had no 5-min usage-threshold event for your wind-down apps. The selection must count for leaves: at least one category token (Social and Entertainment are preselected), or at least 3 apps whose combined daytime use reached 10 min on 3 of the previous 14 days (from a daytime threshold event). Selection changes apply after 7 days. A window with a missing start or end callback is neutral. At most 1 per night. Ladder: 3, 12, 25, 40, 55, 75, 100, 130, 170, 215, 270, 340
- **Phone-free mornings** (`phone_free_mornings`, mornings, from M3): Mornings where your final wake fell between target wake − 30 min and target wake + 15 min (otherwise the morning is neutral and counts nothing), and your morning apps had less than 2 min of use before final wake + 30 min. Measured by one rolling DeviceActivity window from target wake − 30 min to target wake + 45 min, with a 2-min threshold event whose time is recorded and compared with HealthKit's final wake. The same 'counts for leaves' selection rule applies. Alarms, music, maps and phone calls are always allowed. Maple's own reveal stays closed until 30 min after wake, so Maple never competes with this window. At most 1 per day. Ladder: 5, 15, 30, 50, 75, 105, 140, 180, 220
- **Shield nights** (`bedtime_shield_kept`, nights, from M3): Nights where the bedtime shield (ManagedSettings) was active from target bedtime to the end of its schedule, over a selection that counts for leaves, and you never took 'five more minutes' or turned blocking off (ShieldAction logs every unlock). Nights when the shield's selection was edited that day are excluded. At most 1 per night. Ladder: 7, 20, 40, 65, 95, 130, 175, 230
- **Reflection weeks** (`reflection_weeks`, weeks, from M4): Calendar weeks with mood-and-energy check-ins saved on at least 3 different days, counted when the week closes. Every mood counts the same. This replaces daily mood check-ins, so there is no daily tap habit to keep up. Cumulative. Ladder: 3, 8, 14, 22, 33, 46
- **Caffeine curfew** (`caffeine_curfew`, days, from M4): Days with a habit-journal entry recording no caffeine after your curfew (default 8 h before target bedtime, adjustable 6–10 h). Only journaled days are evaluated, and logging late caffeine simply adds nothing. Evaluated the next morning. At most 1 per day. Ladder: 7, 20, 45, 90, 160
- **Wind-down rituals** (`wind_down_rituals`, evenings, from M6): Evenings with a completed wind-down ritual of at least 3 min (breathing, meditation or a journaling prompt), started within 2 h before target bedtime. Revealed the next morning. At most 1 per evening. Ladder: 5, 15, 30, 50, 80, 115, 160
- **Community challenges** (`community_challenges`, challenges, from M9): Completed opt-in community challenges. Up to two are offered each month (for example 'fall asleep on time 12 nights in November' or 'six scroll-free wind-downs this month'). Each is built only from the capped categories above, can be done solo, and is evaluated on device; nothing about you is uploaded. Cumulative. Ladder: 1, 3, 6, 10
